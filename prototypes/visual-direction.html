    About

    I'm a researcher in search and language technologies. I lead the Health Search team at the Australian e-Health Research Centre, CSIRO, and I'm an Associate Professor in Language Technologies at the University of Queensland.

    At its core, my research is about helping people find relevant and reliable health information to make health-related decisions.

    Clinically, that means evidence-based medicine, clinical-trial recruitment and radiology. Technically, it means semantic and neural ranking, injecting medical knowledge into models, and keeping the human searcher in the loop.
